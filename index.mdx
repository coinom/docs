Coinom is a cryptocurrency payment gateway that helps businesses, websites, and applications accept crypto payments with minimal setup.

Whether you're running an online store, a SaaS platform, a billing system, or a custom application, Coinom provides everything you need to start accepting cryptocurrency payments in minutes.

<Info>
  [Create an account](https://app.coinom.com/signup) and [create a merchant](/merchants/create), get your API Key, and start accepting crypto payments — all within a few minutes.
</Info>

## How It Works

1. Create a Coinom account.
2. Create a merchant.
3. Access your API Key.
4. Integrate using our API or plugins.
5. Start accepting cryptocurrency payments.

## Supported Integrations

Coinom provides official integrations for:

- WooCommerce
- WHMCS
- OpenCart
- PrestaShop
- Easy Digital Downloads (EDD)
- And more

You can also integrate Coinom into any website, application, or service using our API.

## Supported Cryptocurrencies

Coinom currently supports:

- Bitcoin (BTC)
- Tether (USDT) — TRC20, BEP20, ERC20
- Ethereum (ETH)
- BNB
- TRON (TRX)
- Dogecoin (DOGE)
- Litecoin (LTC)

---

## Automatic Payment Processing

Coinom automatically handles:

- Blockchain payment verification
- Confirmation tracking
- Payment status updates
- Callback and webhook delivery

This allows you to focus on your business while Coinom manages the payment flow.

## Payment Tracking & History

All payments and transactions are available in your Coinom dashboard.

You can:

- View incoming payments
- Track payment statuses
- Monitor transaction history
- Review payment details
- Access transaction references and blockchain information

This gives you a complete overview of your payment activity from a single dashboard.

## Built-in Wallets & Exchange

Every Coinom account includes built-in wallets for supported cryptocurrencies.

You can:

- Store your crypto assets
- Monitor balances
- Receive payments
- Exchange supported assets instantly

All from a single dashboard.
