Creating a Coinom account only takes a minute and requires no KYC or additional documents.

## Steps to Create an Account

1. Open the Coinom signup page:
   [https://app.coinom.com/signup](https://app.coinom.com/signup)
2. Enter your **Email Address**.
3. Enter your **Password**.
4. Enter the same password again in **Confirm Password**.
5. Check the **I agree to Coinom privacy policy & terms** checkbox.
6. Click **Sign Up**.

## Account Access

Your account will be created instantly after registration.

You can then sign in using the same email address and password you used during signup.

<Info>
  No KYC, identity verification, or additional documents are required to create a Coinom account.
</Info>
